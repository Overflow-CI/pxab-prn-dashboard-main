## DHSR (NC Division of Health Service Regulation)

_Licensure, staffing, facility, survey and plan-of-correction rules under 10A NCAC 27G and 27E, G.S. 131E and the MHLCS requirements._

31 warnings.

- **Step 4: Facility Site Selection & Municipal Zoning Approval**
  - Task: Execute lease or purchase agreement with confidentiality/counseling room partitions
  - Warning: AUDIT TRAP: DHSR returns incomplete applications to the sender without processing, and the Day Program checklist requires the original local zoning approval letter. A signed lease without it stalls licensure while rent runs.
- **Step 4: Facility Site Selection & Municipal Zoning Approval**
  - Task: Request formal Zoning Determination Letter from city/county planning department
  - Warning: AUDIT TRAP: A zoning-district-only letter does not verify the use. DHSR asks for zoning approval or verification that the facility is classified for the intended use, so request the use-specific letter by name.
- **Step 7: Governing Body Appointment & Key Clinical Leadership**
  - Task: Document Board of Directors / Advisory Committee minutes
  - Warning: AUDIT TRAP: 10A NCAC 27G .0201(b) requires governing body minutes to be permanently maintained. Missing minutes are a common licensure survey finding.
- **Step 7: Governing Body Appointment & Key Clinical Leadership**
  - Task: Designate the Facility Director named on the DHSR application and delegate management authority in writing (the license is issued to the entity, not the director)
  - Warning: AUDIT TRAP: The license is issued to the licensee entity, and the governing body remains responsible for policies. Naming a director does not move legal responsibility away from the owners or board.
- **Step 7: Governing Body Appointment & Key Clinical Leadership**
  - Task: Appoint a Clinical Director who is an LCAS or CCS for SAIOP and SACOT, or a full-time QP for peer support (10A NCAC 27G .0104)
  - Warning: AUDIT TRAP: The QP definition in 10A NCAC 27G .0104 is now item (21); the text of .4402 and .4502 still cites (18). Cite the current item number in your policies, and use both rules.
- **Step 7: Governing Body Appointment & Key Clinical Leadership**
  - Task: Draft organizational chart showing lines of clinical and administrative supervision
  - Warning: AUDIT TRAP: 10A NCAC 27G .0201(a)(7)(D) requires written supervision of staff who are not qualified professionals by a QP. A chart without matching supervision logs does not satisfy surveyors.
- **Step 8: LME/MCO Network Need Inquiry (a 27G .0406 Letter of Support is for residential facilities)**
  - Task: Get the plan written network-need or contract-status response (a .0406 Letter of Support is only for residential facilities)
  - Warning: AUDIT TRAP: Do not label a plan email as a .0406 letter of support or submit it as a required DHSR attachment for SAIOP or SACOT. DHSR's application packet places the letter only in the 24-hour residential section.
- **Step 9: Statutory Policies & Procedures Engineering**
  - Task: Draft governance, client rights and grievance procedures (10A NCAC 27G .0201, 27D and 27E)
  - Warning: AUDIT TRAP: Surveyors compare policy text to what staff actually do. 27G .0201(a)(18) requires procedures for review and disposition of grievances, so name the reviewer, set deadlines, and keep a grievance log.
- **Step 9: Statutory Policies & Procedures Engineering**
  - Task: Establish Incident Response Improvement System (IRIS) 72-hour reporting workflow (.0600)
  - Warning: AUDIT TRAP: Deaths of clients served within 90 days before the incident are reportable under .0604(a), and Category A providers send Level III death reports to DHSR within 72 hours (.0604(d)). Train all staff, not only the director.
- **Step 10: Core Clinical & Peer Support Staff Recruitment**
  - Task: Execute signed employment agreements with defined job descriptions
  - Warning: AUDIT TRAP: Surveyors pull personnel files for the signed job description, license verification, HCPR check and training. A job description missing either signature fails .0202(a)(3).
- **Step 10: Core Clinical & Peer Support Staff Recruitment**
  - Task: Execute signed employment agreements with defined job descriptions
  - Warning: AUDIT TRAP: Keep the HCPR confirmation number; DHSR states it is the employer's proof of access, and employers must keep a record of accessing the registries.
- **Step 11: Mandatory Pre-Hire Screenings (HCPR & Exclusion Checks)**
  - Task: Execute and file NC Health Care Personnel Registry (HCPR) check PRIOR to hire date
  - Warning: AUDIT TRAP: The confirmation date must be earlier than the hire date. A search run after the first shift does not satisfy the pre-hire requirement in G.S. 131E-256 or 10A NCAC 27G .0202(b)(4).
- **Step 11: Mandatory Pre-Hire Screenings (HCPR & Exclusion Checks)**
  - Task: Conduct state and national criminal history checks with fingerprints
  - Warning: AUDIT TRAP: MHLCS expects State and national criminal check documentation dated within 180 days of the initial licensure review. Stale checks must be redone.
- **Step 12: CPSS Credential & Lived-Experience Verification**
  - Task: File proof of training: Foundations of Peer Support (60 hours on Track A) or the approved 50-hour course (Track B), plus the 20-hour prerequisite training (3 hours of ethics and 17 related hours)
  - Warning: AUDIT TRAP: Surveyors and payers ask for training documents in the personnel file. A certification number alone does not prove the hours.
- **Step 13: Local Fire Marshal & Sanitation Facility Clearances**
  - Task: Pass annual Fire Marshal inspection with stamped approval certificate
  - Warning: AUDIT TRAP: The NC Fire Code's statewide minimum frequency for business occupancies is every three years, but DHSR's rule requires annual fire and safety compliance. Follow the stricter DHSR rule.
- **Step 14: DHSR MHLCS Initial Licensure Application Submission**
  - Task: Complete DHSR Initial Licensure Application PDF
  - Warning: AUDIT TRAP: Incomplete packets are not processed, and day programs need original zoning approval for the application to move forward. Staff must be in place and the site furnished before a license is issued.
- **Step 14: DHSR MHLCS Initial Licensure Application Submission**
  - Task: Attach the original zoning approval and the fire marshal report, plus a sanitation report if you serve food (an LME/MCO letter applies only to residential facilities)
  - Warning: AUDIT TRAP: The 24-hour residential checklist lists LME/MCO letters and inspection appointments, while the Day Program Checklist (Rev 10-01-25) asks for actual reports. Use the Day Program Checklist.
- **Step 14: DHSR MHLCS Initial Licensure Application Submission**
  - Task: Cross-walk the policy manual to 10A NCAC 27G with the DHSR workbook and keep it ready for your consultant (do not enclose it with the application)
  - Warning: AUDIT TRAP: The consultant checks that policies match the rules and that staff are trained on them. Generic manuals bought online often fail.
- **Step 14: DHSR MHLCS Initial Licensure Application Submission**
  - Task: Prepare the staff roster with verified credentials for the consultant review, and enclose the $265 fee with the application
  - Warning: AUDIT TRAP: Check qualified professional ratios in the rules. SAIOP needs one QP per 12 adults or 6 adolescents, and SACOT one per 10 clients.
- **Step 15: DHSR On-Site Initial Licensure Survey**
  - Task: Host DHSR inspector walkthrough of physical day facility
  - Warning: AUDIT TRAP: SAIOP and SACOT require a DHSR Mental Health License on the Provider Permission Matrix. Do not admit clients or bill before the license is issued. DHSR says issuing a license or MHL number may take up to three weeks after compliance.
- **Step 15: DHSR On-Site Initial Licensure Survey**
  - Task: Provide 100% compliant personnel files with pre-hire HCPR checks
  - Warning: AUDIT TRAP: An HCPR check dated after the hire date, or older than DHSR's 90-day window, is a common citation under 10A NCAC 27G .0202(b)(4). Run it before the offer letter and re-run it if the review slips.
- **Step 15: DHSR On-Site Initial Licensure Survey**
  - Task: Provide 100% compliant personnel files with pre-hire HCPR checks
  - Warning: AUDIT TRAP: DHSR does not accept online-only CPR. Keep proof of the hands-on skills component with the certificate.
- **Step 15: DHSR On-Site Initial Licensure Survey**
  - Task: Demonstrate knowledge of client rights and restrictive intervention policies (10A NCAC 27D and 27E .0101 to .0108; 27G .0209 is the medication rule)
  - Warning: AUDIT TRAP: Citing 27G .0209 as the restraint rule in your manual signals that policies were not checked against the rule text. Cite 27E in the policy and on the DHSR worksheet.
- **Step 15: DHSR On-Site Initial Licensure Survey**
  - Task: Demonstrate knowledge of client rights and restrictive intervention policies (10A NCAC 27D and 27E .0101 to .0108; 27G .0209 is the medication rule)
  - Warning: AUDIT TRAP: DHSR asks for the approved curriculum name and instructor certification for de-escalation and restrictive intervention training. Training without a listed curriculum or certified instructor is a common finding.
- **Step 16: Plan of Correction (POC) & License Issuance**
  - Task: Draft Plan of Correction identifying root cause, systemic change, and monitoring frequency
  - Warning: AUDIT TRAP: A plan that fixes only the cited example is the usual reason DHSR rejects a POC. Show that the same gap cannot recur in other files, staff or sites.
- **Step 16: Plan of Correction (POC) & License Issuance**
  - Task: Draft Plan of Correction identifying root cause, systemic change, and monitoring frequency
  - Warning: AUDIT TRAP: Correction dates count from the survey exit date, not the SOD receipt date. Type A1/A2 is 23 days, Type B is 45 days, standard level is 60 days, and re-cited standard level is 30 days. A follow-up visit verifies the fix.
- **Step 16: Plan of Correction (POC) & License Issuance**
  - Task: Submit POC to DHSR within required deadline
  - Warning: AUDIT TRAP: Missing the 10-day return or the exit-date correction window can lead to enforcement. Administrative penalties for uncorrected deficiencies are described in the DHSR resource manual. Calendar both dates on the day the SOD arrives.
- **Step 16: Plan of Correction (POC) & License Issuance**
  - Task: Receive official DHSR Mental Health / SUD Facility License with service designations
  - Warning: AUDIT TRAP: If the service categories on the license do not match the services you enroll in NCTracks, the claim or enrollment can be denied. Ask DHSR for a corrected license before you submit the NCTracks application.
- **Step 23: 24/7 Crisis Response Protocol & Clinical Training**
  - Task: Publish a rotating 24/7 clinical crisis on-call schedule with LCAS or CCS backup
  - Warning: AUDIT TRAP: Auditors and surveyors ask for proof that crisis response actually ran. A schedule alone is not enough. Keep the call log, the after-hours number test results and the notes written by the on-call clinician.
- **Step 23: 24/7 Crisis Response Protocol & Clinical Training**
  - Task: Document staff training on client rights, confidentiality, and de-escalation
  - Warning: AUDIT TRAP: A bare attendance certificate for restrictive-intervention or de-escalation training is not enough. 10A NCAC 27E .0107 expects competency testing and documentation, so keep the test results. The curriculum must be an approved one.
- **Step 23: 24/7 Crisis Response Protocol & Clinical Training**
  - Task: File signed training completion certificates in personnel records
  - Warning: AUDIT TRAP: Missing or undated certificates are a common DHSR deficiency. Surveyors ask for the personnel file during the first-day record pull. Keep files ready on site or retrievable immediately from the EHR.

## NCTracks (NC Medicaid claims and enrollment system)

_Enrollment, EFT, billing codes, provider records and the enrollment and claims rules that run through NCTracks._

17 warnings.

- **Step 6: Dedicated Business Bank Account & EFT Setup**
  - Task: Obtain a bank letter verifying routing and account numbers for LME/MCO EFT forms (NCTracks takes EFT details online)
  - Warning: AUDIT TRAP: Transposed digits cause EFT pre-note failures and delayed payment. Read numbers from the bank letter rather than retyping from memory.
- **Step 8: LME/MCO Network Need Inquiry (a 27G .0406 Letter of Support is for residential facilities)**
  - Task: Submit a program summary and county location to the Tailored Plan that covers your county and ask whether it is accepting new providers
  - Warning: AUDIT TRAP: NCTracks enrollment does not put you in a network. A clinician or site cannot bill a plan until it is both enrolled in NCTracks and contracted with that plan.
- **Step 17: NC HealthConnex (HIE) Participation Agreement & Integration**
  - Task: File written confirmation of your connection status from HIEA (no source found requires proof before billing; confirm with your plans)
  - Warning: AUDIT TRAP: No source found requires filing proof of connection with NCTracks before billing. Do not tell staff that billing is blocked or allowed on this basis until you confirm with HIEA, NC Medicaid and your Tailored Plan.
- **Step 18: NCTracks Organization Provider Enrollment Application**
  - Task: Obtain NCID for designated Office Administrator
  - Warning: AUDIT TRAP: If the OA leaves, only an owner or managing employee on file can replace the OA. Adding a second managing employee now prevents a stuck account.
- **Step 18: NCTracks Organization Provider Enrollment Application**
  - Task: Complete NCTracks Organization Enrollment Application
  - Warning: AUDIT TRAP: Taxonomy 251S00000X providers must have at least one Service selected on the Provider Permission Matrix. NC Medicaid extended the deadline to October 1, 2026, so confirm that Peer Support, SAIOP and SACOT show on your record.
- **Step 18: NCTracks Organization Provider Enrollment Application**
  - Task: Select taxonomy 251S00000X and add each service (Peer Support, SAIOP, SACOT) as the Provider Permission Matrix lists them
  - Warning: AUDIT TRAP: Enrolling under 251S00000X without a service selected is no longer allowed. NC Medicaid set October 1, 2026 as the deadline for existing providers, and a new application needs services chosen up front.
- **Step 18: NCTracks Organization Provider Enrollment Application**
  - Task: Sign the W-9 attestation, enter EFT details online, and upload only the documents the application asks for (NCTracks verifies the DHSR license)
  - Warning: AUDIT TRAP: A bank name, account title or EIN that does not match the legal entity delays EFT setup and payment. Use the same name as on your W-9 and IRS records.
- **Step 19: Moderate/High Risk Fingerprinting & Site Screening**
  - Task: Schedule LiveScan fingerprinting at an approved site after the NCTracks notice arrives, before the deadline in the notice
  - Warning: AUDIT TRAP: Fingerprint results from other agencies cannot be reused. Only Medicare fingerprint results through PECOS are accepted, so a prior DHHS or DSS background check will not satisfy NCTracks.
- **Step 19: Moderate/High Risk Fingerprinting & Site Screening**
  - Task: Upload the NCTracks-generated fingerprint Release of Information forms in the portal (mailed forms are not processed)
  - Warning: AUDIT TRAP: Forms mailed to NCTracks or to the address on the form's Agency Section are not processed. Only the portal upload counts.
- **Step 19: Moderate/High Risk Fingerprinting & Site Screening**
  - Task: Pass NCTracks physical site visit verification
  - Warning: AUDIT TRAP: If the person at the visit cannot show basic knowledge of the enrolled services, the visit is recorded as a fail, which leads to automatic termination of participation per NC Medicaid's March 2026 provider notice.
- **Step 20: NCTracks Approval & Effective Date Alignment**
  - Task: Verify billing effective date aligns with credentials
  - Warning: AUDIT TRAP: Services delivered before the effective date shown on the provider record are not billable. A back-date request cannot precede the license or accreditation begin date on file.
- **Step 20: NCTracks Approval & Effective Date Alignment**
  - Task: Have each individually enrolled clinician Office Administrator submit a Manage Change Request to affiliate to your organization NPI (confirm with NC Medicaid whether agency staff must be enrolled)
  - Warning: AUDIT TRAP: Your organization cannot create or change the affiliation. Only the Office Administrator of the individual's record can. The older Medicaid Bulletin says affiliation-related claims deny if not fixed within the stated period.
- **Step 20: NCTracks Approval & Effective Date Alignment**
  - Task: Test electronic claims submission and ERA 835 delivery
  - Warning: AUDIT TRAP: Medicaid Direct claims go to NCTracks, but Tailored Plan and Standard Plan claims go to the plan, not NCTracks. Test each plan's claim route separately.
- **Step 22: EHR Clinical Setup & Service Note (RMDM) Scrubber**
  - Task: Configure 3-hr daily minimum for SAIOP (H0015, one event per day) and 4-hr daily minimum for SACOT (H2035, one unit per hour)
  - Warning: AUDIT TRAP: The SACOT code is H2035, not H2036. A wrong code will deny or be paid incorrectly.
- **Step 25: National Accreditation Roadmap & NCTracks 5-Yr Reverification**
  - Task: Calendar NCTracks 5-year revalidation (first notice 70 days out, reminders at 50, 20 and 5 days; a missed deadline means suspension, then termination)
  - Warning: AUDIT TRAP: Missing the deadline can lead to suspension and then termination of Medicaid enrollment. Claims stop paying during suspension.
- **Step 30: Program Integrity, Provider Screening & Funding Risk**
  - Task: Calendar NCTracks updates for ownership, address, license, NPI and taxonomy changes, with the reporting deadline for each
  - Warning: AUDIT TRAP: NC Medicaid says that missing the 30-day deadline for disclosing an adverse action will terminate your NCTracks provider record.
- **Step 30: Program Integrity, Provider Screening & Funding Risk**
  - Task: Calendar NCTracks updates for ownership, address, license, NPI and taxonomy changes, with the reporting deadline for each
  - Warning: AUDIT TRAP: Errors in a reverification application cannot be corrected after you submit it. A rejected application after the suspension date automatically terminates your enrollment.

- **Step 6: Dedicated Business Bank Account & EFT Setup**
  - Task: Establish a business checking account in the exact legal entity name and keep a voided check for LME/MCO EFT forms
  - Warning: AUDIT TRAP: The account holder name should match the legal business name and EIN on the W-9. Alliance's vendor packet asks for legal name and TIN matching its records, and mismatches can hold setup.

  - Warning: AUDIT TRAP: Trillium's May 8, 2026 bulletin expects zero late reports: Level 3 incidents need notice to Trillium verbally or by email within 24 hours plus IRIS within 72 hours; staff-allegation incidents go into IRIS within 24 hours with the investigation uploaded in five working days. Other plans may differ.

  - Task: Submit each plan clinician roster or enrollment form with NCTracks records (no plan page found asks for CAQH IDs; confirm with each plan)
  - Warning: AUDIT TRAP: Alliance requires professional liability and general liability of at least $1,000,000 per occurrence and $3,000,000 aggregate, plus workers' compensation. Missing certificates stall contracts.
- **Step 21: Regional Tailored Plan / LME-MCO Network Participation**
  - Task: Establish Electronic Data Interchange (EDI) testing with plan clearinghouses
  - Warning: AUDIT TRAP: Alliance's billing-loop address, including zip+4, must exactly match the site address in NCTracks, or claims map to the wrong site and deny.
- **Step 21: Regional Tailored Plan / LME-MCO Network Participation**
  - Task: Complete Utilization Management (UM) portal training for prior authorizations
  - Warning: AUDIT TRAP: You may not bill units beyond those authorized by the plan. Vaya Partners states this directly.
- **Step 26: H.R. 1 Impact Review & Compliance Owner**
  - Task: Subscribe to NC Medicaid bulletins and each Tailored Plan provider notice list for H.R. 1 updates
  - Warning: AUDIT TRAP: Tailored Plan bulletins can change contract or billing requirements. Keep a dated log showing you received and acted on each notice.

## OIG (HHS Office of Inspector General)

_Exclusion screening, excluded-person billing, and civil monetary penalties._

5 warnings.

- **Step 9: Statutory Policies & Procedures Engineering**
  - Task: Draft Fraud, Waste & Abuse (FWA) / False Claims Act compliance plan
  - Warning: AUDIT TRAP: Billing for or employing an excluded person can lead to repayment and civil penalties. Keep dated screening printouts for every employee, contractor and owner; plans ask for proof.
- **Step 11: Mandatory Pre-Hire Screenings (HCPR & Exclusion Checks)**
  - Task: Screen staff and 5%+ owners against OIG LEIE, SAM.gov, and NC Exclusion database
  - Warning: AUDIT TRAP: Medicaid will not pay for items or services furnished, ordered or prescribed by an excluded person. OIG's 2013 bulletin cites civil monetary penalties of up to $10,000 per item or service, so one unscreened hire can create overpayment liability.
- **Step 11: Mandatory Pre-Hire Screenings (HCPR & Exclusion Checks)**
  - Task: Establish recurring monthly OIG exclusion audit calendar
  - Warning: AUDIT TRAP: OIG says it has no required check frequency but advises routine checks. CMS guidance recommends monthly checks, and auditors and MCOs treat monthly as the norm. A missing month is a gap you must explain.
- **Step 25: National Accreditation Roadmap & NCTracks 5-Yr Reverification**
  - Task: Calendar monthly OIG/SAM exclusion screenings and 2-year CPSS renewals
  - Warning: AUDIT TRAP: Payments for services by an excluded person can be recouped, and civil penalties can apply. Monthly screening is the standard auditors expect, and gaps in your log are a finding.
- **Step 30: Program Integrity, Provider Screening & Funding Risk**
  - Task: Add a monthly screen of every owner, manager and rendering provider against the OIG exclusion list, SAM.gov and the NC Medicaid exclusion list (a participation agreement requirement)
  - Warning: AUDIT TRAP: Payments for services ordered or furnished by an excluded person are overpayments, and OIG can impose civil money penalties on whoever hired them. Monthly screening matches OIG's monthly LEIE updates.

## NC Medicaid policy (Clinical Coverage Policies, bulletins and 10A NCAC 22J)

_Service rules in the Clinical Coverage Policies (CCP 8G, 8A-12, 8A-13), NC Medicaid bulletins and the Medicaid provider rules._

25 warnings.

- **Step 8: LME/MCO Network Need Inquiry (a 27G .0406 Letter of Support is for residential facilities)**
  - Task: List the services you plan to offer (Peer Support, SAIOP, SACOT) and ask whether each is a current network need in your county
  - Warning: AUDIT TRAP: Match the service names, ages and hours in your request to the CCPs. SACOT is for ages 18 and older at ASAM Level 2.5 with 20 or more hours weekly; SAIOP adult programming is 9 to 19 hours weekly. Mismatches surface at credentialing and audit.
- **Step 8: LME/MCO Network Need Inquiry (a 27G .0406 Letter of Support is for residential facilities)**
  - Task: List the services you plan to offer (Peer Support, SAIOP, SACOT) and ask whether each is a current network need in your county
  - Warning: AUDIT TRAP: CCP 8G bars Peer Support during the same time of day a beneficiary is in SAIOP or SACOT, so schedule peers outside program hours if the same clients are enrolled in all three.
- **Step 9: Statutory Policies & Procedures Engineering**
  - Task: Integrate RMDM service note requirements cited in CCP 8G, 8A-12 and 8A-13
  - Warning: AUDIT TRAP: The ten-element list is not an official label. The manual has ten content bullets plus authentication plus CCP additions, and CCP 8G lists eleven elements (a through k). Build the note template from both, not from a checklist of ten.
- **Step 9: Statutory Policies & Procedures Engineering**
  - Task: Integrate RMDM service note requirements cited in CCP 8G, 8A-12 and 8A-13
  - Warning: AUDIT TRAP: Each note needs its own newly composed evidence of the service. The manual says notes copied from an earlier date or another person's record, or near-identical to a prior note, are not acceptable as individualized notes.
- **Step 10: Core Clinical & Peer Support Staff Recruitment**
  - Task: Verify SACOT staffing: at least 1 clinical staff for every 10 or fewer beneficiaries in group counseling (CCP 8A-13 section 6.2)
  - Warning: AUDIT TRAP: The 1:10 ratio applies per group session, based on the census at that time. Unsigned rosters or groups over 10 with one facilitator are a typical finding.
- **Step 10: Core Clinical & Peer Support Staff Recruitment**
  - Task: Verify Peer Support staffing: 1 QP per 8 CPSS, and CPSS to beneficiary ratios of 1:15 individual and 1:12 group (CCP 8G section 6.2.1)
