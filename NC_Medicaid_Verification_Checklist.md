# Verification checklist for the NC Medicaid BH Dashboard

Prepared 2026-10-19. This covers every fact in the app that the research could not confirm on an official page, or where sources conflicted.

**Why this list exists.** The researched instructions, fees, dates and contacts in the app came from a Perplexity research pass. They were not independently checked, because the NC government sites were blocked in the build environment. Contacts and policy details can change, especially after the Vaya Health and Partners merger on Oct 1, 2026.

**This is research, not legal advice.** Items marked ⚖️ need a healthcare attorney. Items marked 💰 affect your budget.

## How to use this list

1. Work top to bottom. Part A holds the decisions that can cost you the most if wrong.
2. Ask by **email first** wherever an email is listed. You get a written answer you can keep.
3. If you call, say the script, then **send a one-line email afterwards** that restates the answer and asks them to reply "correct".
4. Record every answer in the log at the bottom: date, name, reference number, answer.
5. When an answer differs from the app, tell me and I'll update the app.

**Tips for every call**
- Have your NPI, legal entity name and EIN ready if you have them. Say "we are a new provider, not yet enrolled" up front.
- Ask for a **name and a reference or ticket number**.
- Say "can you send that to me in writing?" at the end.

# Part A. Decide-first questions

These change what you build, where, and when. Settle them before you sign a lease or pay a fee.

## A1. ☐ Accreditation deadline for SAIOP and SACOT: 1 year or 3 years? ⚖️

- **What the app says:** Plan for 1 year from NC Medicaid enrollment. Peer support (8G) is 1 year in every source.
- **The conflict:** G.S. 122C-81(d) and CCP 8A-12 and 8A-13 section 6.1 say one year. The April 16, 2026 bulletin, the May 28, 2026 NCTracks notice and the Aug 16, 2026 Provider Permission Matrix say three years for SAIOP and SACOT.
- **Also unclear:** when the clock starts (NC Medicaid enrollment of the provider, or enrollment of the specific service in NCTracks), and whether **CQL** is an accepted accreditor. The Aug 16 matrix lists it. The April bulletin lists only COA, Joint Commission and CARF.
- **Who:** NC Medicaid Provider Ombudsman (866-304-7062, email preferred), or the NCTracks Call Center (800-688-6696).
- **Email to send** (to Medicaid.ProviderOmbudsman@dhhs.nc.gov):
  > Subject: Written clarification needed: accreditation deadline for new SAIOP and SACOT providers
  >
  > We are a new provider planning to enroll for CCP 8A-12 (SAIOP) and 8A-13 (SACOT), and possibly 8G. G.S. 122C-81(d) and CCP 8A-12 and 8A-13 section 6.1 say national accreditation is due within one year of enrollment. The April 16, 2026 bulletin and the August 16, 2026 Provider Permission Matrix say within three years. Please confirm in writing: (1) Which deadline does NCTracks enforce for SAIOP and SACOT? (2) Does the clock start at NC Medicaid enrollment as a provider, or at enrollment of the individual service in NCTracks? (3) Is CQL an accepted accrediting body for 8G, 8A-12 and 8A-13, as the August 16 matrix suggests, or only COA, the Joint Commission and CARF? Thank you.
- **Phone script:** "Hi, I'm a new provider planning to enroll for SAIOP and SACOT. The statute says accreditation is due in one year and your Permission Matrix says three years. Which one does NCTracks enforce, and when does the clock start? Also, is CQL accepted? Can you send me that in writing?"
- **Write down:** the deadline, the start event, the accepted accreditors, and the name and reference number.
- **Until you hear back:** keep planning for 1 year.

## A2. ☐ Will each plan actually contract with you, in your county? 💰

- **What the app says:** Peer support is a closed network for all three plans. SAIOP and SACOT show "Taking applications", which only means applications are accepted, not that a contract is promised.
- **Specific gaps:** Trillium lists SAIOP and SACOT needs only in Dare and Hyde. Vaya Partners has no service-needs page, and it is unconfirmed whether it takes new requests in the former Partners counties. Alliance replies on need within 5 business days of a complete request.
- **Who:** the network team of each plan that covers your counties (check the app's Regional contacts panel for which plans apply).
- **Alliance** (NetworkRelations@AllianceHealthPlan.org, 855-759-9700):
  > We are a new provider planning to open [SAIOP / SACOT / peer support] in [county]. Your service-needs page shows [open / no listing] for this service. Is there a network need for it in [county]? If so, what do you need to start a Provider Application request, and what is the expected timeline from application to contract? Is peer support (8G) reviewed case by case under the July 2026 mandatory closed network?
- **Trillium** (NetworkServicesSupport@TrilliumNC.org, 1-855-250-1539):
  > We plan to open [service] in [county]. Your Current Service Needs page lists SAIOP and SACOT needs only in Dare and Hyde counties. Is there any network need in [county]? Are you accepting applications for peer support, or is it fully closed? What is the process and timeline to be considered?
- **Vaya Partners** (ProviderEnrollment@VayaPartners.org, 1-855-432-9139):
  > We plan to open [service] in [county], which was formerly in the [Vaya Health / Partners] area. Are you accepting new Provider Contract Request Forms for peer support, SAIOP and SACOT in [county]? Where do you post closed-network service needs? Is the network treated the same in the former Partners counties as in the former Vaya counties? Do you accept applications before our NCTracks enrollment is complete?
- **Write down:** yes or no per service per county, the stated need, the timeline, and who said it.

## A3. ☐ Does a peer-support-only office need any DHSR step or inspection? ⚖️

- **What the app says:** No DHSR license for peer support (DHSR's Feb 10, 2026 new-provider slide and the Aug 16, 2026 matrix). Steps 14–16 now show only for SAIOP and SACOT. The "not licensable" reading of G.S. 122C-3(14) is the researcher's inference, and DHSR's own slide is the direct evidence.
- **Who:** DHSR Mental Health Licensure and Certification Section, 919-855-3795.
- **Phone script:** "Hi, we're planning a Peer Support Services provider under CCP 8G, with an office where staff do paperwork and meet clients, but no day or residential program. Can you confirm in writing that this does not need a DHSR mental health license, a DHSR site walkthrough, or a licensure application? And is there anything DHSR requires of us for the office itself?"
- **Also ask:** "Do the G.S. 122C-80 criminal record check rules apply to us, or do we use the Health Care Personnel Registry only?"
- **Write down:** confirmation, the person's name, and a way to get it in writing. If they won't put it in writing, ask a healthcare attorney to confirm.

## A4. ☐ Rural Health Innovation Fund: does a new behavioral health provider qualify? 💰

- **What the app says:** Year 1 is limited to primary care providers, FQHCs, community health centers and safety-net providers. The deadline is **Nov 16, 2026, 11:59 p.m.** A page on ncbroadband.gov lists behavioral health providers more broadly.
- **Who:** RHIF@nc.gov (919-754-6733 for NCDIT Broadband).
- **Email to send:**
  > Subject: RHIF year 1 eligibility for a new behavioral health provider
  >
  > We are a new North Carolina behavioral health provider planning to offer peer support (CCP 8G), SAIOP (8A-12) and SACOT (8A-13) in [county]. We are not an FQHC or primary care practice. Is a behavioral health provider eligible in the first Rural Health Innovation Fund round that closes November 16, 2026? If not, when is the next round, and is there any other NCRHTP funding open to us? Thank you.
- **Do this soon.** The deadline is weeks away.

# Part B. NCTracks and NC Medicaid enrollment

Contact for all of Part B: NCTracks Call Center, 800-688-6696, unless noted.

## B1. ☐ Taxonomy and enrollment path
- **App says:** Use taxonomy 251S00000X (Agencies, Community/Behavioral Health) for all three services. High risk: site visit, owner fingerprinting and the federal application fee ($750 in 2026).
- **Phone script:** "We're enrolling a new organization for peer support, SAIOP and SACOT. Is 251S00000X the right taxonomy for all three? Does it put us in the high-risk category with a site visit, fingerprinting and the federal application fee? What's the current fee amount?"
- **Record:** taxonomy, risk level, fee amount.

## B2. ☐ Owner fingerprinting: price, timing and process 💰
- **App says:** Owners with 5% or more ownership are fingerprinted. The price varies by location and is NOT VERIFIED. The 30-day window is not stated in the Aug 11, 2026 FAQ.
- **Phone script:** "After we submit, we'll receive a fingerprinting notice. How many days do we have to complete it, and what happens if we miss it? Is there any fee collected by NCTracks or the SBI, separate from the fingerprint location's own fee? Which locations do you recommend near [city]?"
- **Then call the chosen location** and ask: "What do you charge for NCTracks provider fingerprinting, and do I need an appointment?"
- **Record:** deadline in days, fees, chosen location.

## B3. ☐ PCG site visit and training
- **App says:** PCG schedules the site visit and training. Missing either auto-denies the application. What the visitor checks is NOT VERIFIED.
- **Who:** PCG, 877-522-1057. Backup: NCTracks.
- **Phone script:** "We're a new provider applying under taxonomy 251S00000X. When will the site visit be scheduled after we submit? What does the visitor check, and what documents should we have on site? Is the new-provider training a separate deadline? What happens if we reschedule?"
- **Record:** lead time, checklist, how to track the visit.

## B4. ☐ Total enrollment timeline
- **App says:** Typical total time is NOT VERIFIED. The research notes only that MCRs take about 10 business days per an NCTracks FAQ; it does not say what MCR stands for or whether it applies to you.
- **Phone script:** "What's the current average processing time for a new organization enrollment with a site visit, from submission to approval?"

## B5. ☐ Electronic funds transfer (EFT) at enrollment
- **App says:** Bank details are keyed in NCTracks. Whether a voided check or bank letter is uploaded is NOT VERIFIED (source is a 2022 guide).
- **Phone script:** "At the EFT step for a new organization, do we upload a voided check or bank letter, or just key in the routing and account numbers? Is a DHSR license upload ever requested?"

## B6. ☐ Who must be enrolled or affiliated: rendering staff
- **App says:** Whether QPs, peer support specialists and associate-level staff must be enrolled and affiliated in NCTracks for 8G, 8A-12 and 8A-13 claims is NOT VERIFIED.
- **Who:** NC Medicaid Contact Center 888-245-0179, plus each plan's provider relations.
- **Phone script:** "For a peer support agency billing under CCP 8G, do the individual peer support specialists need their own NPI and NCTracks enrollment or affiliation, or does the agency bill as the rendering provider? Does the same answer apply to SAIOP and SACOT staff?"

## B7. ☐ Credentialing: NCTracks versus each plan
- **App says:** NCTracks verifies credentials, and plans decide network entry and sign contracts. CAQH requirements for agencies are NOT VERIFIED. The NC Medicaid Credentialing Committee start date is NOT VERIFIED.
- **Phone script (each plan):** "After our NCTracks approval, do you perform any separate credentialing? Do you require CAQH IDs or a roster attestation for an agency? What forms and roster template do you use?"

## B8. ☐ Does the effective date follow NCTracks approval?
- **App says:** Step 20 aligns your first billing date with NCTracks approval and plan contracts.
- **Phone script:** "If NCTracks approves us on a certain date but our plan contract is signed later, what is our billing effective date? Can claims be backdated?"

# Part D. Background checks and screening

## D1. ☐ State and national check fees for employees 💰
- **App says:** The SBI form lists **$38** per fingerprint card for the national check. The state-only fee is capped at $14 by G.S. 143B-939, but the current charged fee and the cap's current section number are NOT VERIFIED.
- **Who:** SBI Criminal Information and Identification Section (look up the current number on the SBI website).
- **Phone script:** "What is the current fee for a state-only criminal record check and for a state and national fingerprint check for a health care provider? Is the $38 form the current revision?"
- **Also ask (⚖️):** "We are not yet licensed. How can a new provider run checks on hires before we have a DHSR license?" Per the research, pre-license state checks go through a private vendor, and national checks wait until licensure. Confirm with a healthcare attorney.

## D2. ☐ HCPR and exclusion checks
- **App says:** Health Care Personnel Registry (HCPR) checks apply to community MH/DD/SA providers, licensed or not. Monthly LEIE and SAM screening comes from the NCDHHS participation agreement.
- **Who:** DHSR Health Care Personnel Registry, and each plan.
- **Phone script (Alliance):** "Your provider manual lists the Social Security Death Master File. Which access method do you accept?"
- **Phone script (Vaya Partners):** "What provider screening and reporting rules apply to us (LEIE, SAM and others), and where in your manual are they?"
