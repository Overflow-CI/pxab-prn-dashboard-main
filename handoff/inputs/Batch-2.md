## C) CORRECTIONS

- **Letter of Support scope.** The app says it is a mandatory DHSR attachment for all three services. Rule [10A NCAC 27G .0406](http://reports.oah.state.nc.us/ncac/title%2010a%20-%20health%20and%20human%20services/chapter%2027%20-%20mental%20health,%20community%20facilities%20and%20services/subchapter%20g/subchapter%20g%20rules.html) is titled "Letter of Support Required for Licensure of Residential Facilities." The [DHSR packet](https://info.ncdhhs.gov/dhsr/mhlcs/pdf/mhiapp.pdf) lists the letter only in its 24-hour residential section. The [DHSR establish page](https://info.ncdhhs.gov/dhsr/mhlcs/establish.html) tells day programs only to contact the LME/MCO for need and contracting.
- **Number of LME/MCOs.** The app lists four plans. Vaya Health and Partners merged on October 1, 2026 as Vaya Partners, leaving Alliance, Trillium and Vaya Partners ([NC DHHS directory](https://www.ncdhhs.gov/providers/lme-mco-directory)). Partners also paused new contracting requests and licensure letters of support effective August 1, 2026.[providers.partnersbhm.org](https://providers.partnersbhm.org/partners-pauses-new-contracting-requests/?share_frome=aipt)
- **Peer Support network status (new).** Peer Support became a mandatory closed-network service for all prepaid health plans on July 7, 2026 under Session Law 2026-41, per NC Medicaid. Alliance says July 2, 2026; NC Medicaid is the more authoritative source.[alliancehealthplan.org](https://www.alliancehealthplan.org/providers/network/become-a-provider/provider-enrollment/?share_frome=aipt)[medicaid.ncdhhs.gov](https://medicaid.ncdhhs.gov/providers/provider-playbook-medicaid-managed-care/fact-sheets/mandatory-closed-networks?share_frome=aipt)
- **Peer Support ratio.** The app says a 1:6 QP-to-CPSS ratio. [CCP 8G](https://medicaid.ncdhhs.gov/8g-peer-support-services/open), amended September 1, 2026, says QP-to-CPSS is 1:8, CPSS-to-beneficiary is 1:15 (correct in the app), and the group ratio is 1:12. The program must be under a full-time QP.
- **CCP 8G amendment items the app omits.** The September 1, 2026 amendment requires the program to be integrated with CCA, psychotherapy and medication management, or a signed MOU or MOA. It also bars Peer Support at the same time of day as SAIOP or SACOT.
- **SACOT staffing wording.** The app says "Qualified Professionals for group therapy." [CCP 8A-13](https://medicaid.ncdhhs.gov/media/15769/download) (amended January 1, 2026) uses Licensed Clinical Staff and Certified Clinical Staff, at one per 10 or fewer beneficiaries during group counseling. Rule .4502(b) still uses Qualified Professional at 1:10, so satisfy both.
- **Clinical Supervisor on-site percentages.** The app omits them. Rule .4402 requires 50% of SAIOP hours and .4502 requires 90% of SACOT hours. The CCPs also allow a Clinical Supervisor Intern and a split of the role between two staff.
- **"Ten-element" service note and "APSM 45-2".** The [July 2025 DHHS manual](https://www.ncdhhs.gov/rmdmjuly-2025/download?attachment) (effective July 8, 2025) lists ten content bullets, then authentication, then CCP-specific additions. CCP 8G lists eleven elements (a through k). The manual text no longer prints the label "APSM 45-2".
- **Policy "review tool".** The DHSR document is the [Policy and Procedure Workbook](https://info.ncdhhs.gov/dhsr/mhlcs/pdf/PolicyProcedure-Workbook.pdf) (rev 3-20-25). The packet says not to submit policies with the application.
- **IRIS timelines.** "72-hour" is right for Level II (except deaths) and Level III reports, but the app omits the 24-hour internal review team and the 24-hour HCPR report. IRIS stands for Incident Response Improvement System, not "critical incident" ([rule .0603 and .0604](http://reports.oah.state.nc.us/ncac/title%2010a%20-%20health%20and%20human%20services/chapter%2027%20-%20mental%20health,%20community%20facilities%20and%20services/subchapter%20g/subchapter%20g%20rules.html); [Trillium bulletin](https://www.trilliumhealthresources.org/sites/default/files/docs/Network-Communication-Bulletin/466-Network-Communication-Bulletin.pdf)).
- **42 CFR Part 2 "modernized".** The revised rule required compliance by February 16, 2026, so it is already mandatory ([HHS fact sheet](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/regulatory-initiatives/fact-sheet-42-cfr-part-2-final-rule/index.html), [eCFR](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/chapter-I/subchapter-A/part-2)).
- **Timing.** The "2–6 weeks" estimate for a letter is unverified. Under .0406(e) the LME must respond to a residential request within five business days, and Alliance also states five business days for provider applications.
- **Resource link.** pss.unc.edu is the NC CPSS program only. LCAS and CCS credentials are verified through NCASPPB.[pss.unc.edu](https://pss.unc.edu/?share_frome=aipt)[ncsappb.org](https://www.ncsappb.org/credentialing/licensed-clinical-addiction-specialist/?share_frome=aipt)
