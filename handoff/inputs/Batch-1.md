## CORRECTIONS

- **Step 7, Clinical Director "meeting QP standards".** QP alone is not enough for SAIOP or SACOT leadership. The rules require an LCAS or CCS on site at least 50 percent of hours for SAIOP (10A NCAC 27G .4402) and 90 percent for SACOT (.4502). CCP 8A-12 and 8A-13 (amended Jan 1, 2026) also allow a Clinical Supervisor Intern. QP applies to direct care staff and the CCP 8G program supervisor ([27G rules](http://reports.oah.state.nc.us/ncac/title%2010a%20-%20health%20and%20human%20services/chapter%2027%20-%20mental%20health,%20community%20facilities%20and%20services/subchapter%20g/subchapter%20g%20rules.html), [CCP 8A-12](https://medicaid.ncdhhs.gov/media/15767/download), [CCP 8A-13](https://medicaid.ncdhhs.gov/media/15769/download)).
- **QP item number.** The QP definition is now item (21) of 10A NCAC 27G .0104 after the Jan 1, 2024 amendment. The .4402 and .4502 text still cites item (18).
- **"Facility Director (named on license)".** The DHSR application names the Facility Director on the form, but the license is issued to the licensee entity. I found no source making the director the "legal responsible party" ([DHSR application](https://info.ncdhhs.gov/dhsr/mhlcs/pdf/mhiapp.pdf)).
- **Step 5, insurance "required by DHSR".** The DHSR application packet does not mention insurance. The $1M/$3M limits come from LME/MCO terms, for example [Alliance](https://www.alliancehealthplan.org/providers/network/qa/insurance/), and the DHHS model contract leaves limits to each LME/PIHP.
- **Step 5, "name the agency as primary insured".** Your agency is the named insured. The plan is the certificate holder, or additional insured where required. Primary and non-contributory is an endorsement, not a role.
- **Step 6, "voided check for NCTracks EFT".** NCTracks takes EFT data keyed into the enrollment application or portal, and the sources I read do not require a check or bank letter ([NCTracks job aid](https://www.nctracks.nc.gov/content/dam/jcr:b77caef7-8592-4d5a-9c65-9798ca6a13f2/JA_PRV531_Add-Updt%20EFT%20Info_W2.0.1.pdf)). The voided check or bank letter is used by LME/MCOs such as Alliance and Trillium.
- **Step 4, $50-$200 zoning fee and 3-6 weeks.** Neither is verified statewide. Durham charges $58 and $28 for its two letter types ([Durham](https://www.durhamnc.gov/4856/Verification-Letters)).
- **Step 4, main resource.** The NCACC link covers counties only, and city zoning is often municipal. I could not open that page, so it is not verified.
- **Step 4, SAIOP and SACOT building class.** DHSR lists Group B Business Occupancy for adult SAIOP and SACOT. Adolescent day programs cannot be in a Business Occupancy.
- **Step 4, CCP 8G.** Peer Support is not on DHSR's licensed-program list, so DHSR zoning and inspection paperwork may not apply to a peer-support-only site.
- **Step 4, accessibility code.** The OSFM page shows the 2018 NC Building Code as currently effective. The 2024 code's effective date is not settled.
- **Steps 5, 6 and 7, main-resource links.** The NCDOI, FDIC and oah.nc.gov links are generic and do not state the requirements.
- **LME/MCO changes.** Vaya and Partners began combined claims processing as Vaya Partners on Oct 1, 2026, and Trillium's unified claims platform took effect the same day ([Vaya Partners](https://providers.vayahealth.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/8_24_26_ProviderMergerInfoSession.pdf), [Trillium](https://www.trilliumhealthresources.org/sites/default/files/docs/Provider-documents/Claims/Trillium-Unified-Claims-Platform-Presentation-082726.pdf)).
- **CCP versions.** CCP 8A-12 and 8A-13 were amended Jan 1, 2026 and require national accreditation within one year of enrollment. CCP 8G was amended Sept 1, 2026.
