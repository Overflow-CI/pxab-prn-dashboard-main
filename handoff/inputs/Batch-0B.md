## A. Other provider to-do items (prioritized)

1. **Find out whether your SAIOP/SACOT counts as a "treatment program" (now).** The federal definition covers only nonprofits or public community mental health centers (7 U.S.C. 2012(h); [IFC](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-06-03/html/2026-11094.htm)). If the provider is a for-profit LLC, clients may need the medical frailty route instead. Ask NC Medicaid how it will apply this.[law.cornell.edu](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/7/2012?share_frome=aipt)
2. **Code diagnoses and services precisely (now through Jan 1, 2027).** NC flags medical frailty from 12 months of claims ([42 CFR 435.557 via the IFC](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-06-03/html/2026-11094.htm)).
   - The draft Tier 1 code list includes H0015 (SAIOP), H2035 (SACOT) and F1x.20 dependence codes.
   - It does not include H0038 (peer support), F1x.21 "in remission" codes or F10.10 ([NC draft code list](https://medicaid.ncdhhs.gov/media/16283/download?attachment)).
   - Submit comments by Nov 5, 2026.[medicaid.ncdhhs.gov](https://medicaid.ncdhhs.gov/meetings-notices/proposed-medicaid-policies?share_frome=aipt)
3. **Set up a DSS-8209 attestation workflow (2027).** It needs a licensed clinician's signature and a Part 2 consent. Self-attestation tightens in 2028, so clients will need documentation ([NC draft attestation](https://medicaid.ncdhhs.gov/media/16282/download?attachment); NCDHHS comment letter).[ncdhhs.gov](https://www.ncdhhs.gov/north-carolina-dhhs-ifc-comment-letter/open?share_frome=aipt)
4. **Check eligibility at every visit**, as the draft policy requires. Watch for six-month renewal dates, the Oct 1, 2026 immigrant losses, and shorter retroactive coverage ([NC draft policy HR-1](https://medicaid.ncdhhs.gov/media/16285/download?attachment)).
5. **Plan for state budget and rate risk** from the provider-tax and directed-payment limits (FY2028 and later) and from the expansion triggers ([P.L. 119-21](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/PLAW-119publ21/html/PLAW-119publ21.htm)).
6. **Payroll changes from the tax side of the law.** W-2s must report qualified overtime (relevant for peer staff). The Form 1099 threshold changes to $2,000 starting 2026 (NOT VERIFIED on irs.gov; confirm with a CPA).
