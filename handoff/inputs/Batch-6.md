## CORRECTIONS

- **"24/7/365 first-responder coverage":** The current policies (effective January 1, 2026, Section 6.1) require crisis response 24 hours a day, seven days a week, in person, by telehealth or by phone based on client need. Neither policy uses the phrase first-responder or 365. [CCP 8A-12](https://medicaid.ncdhhs.gov/media/15767/download?attachment), [CCP 8A-13](https://medicaid.ncdhhs.gov/media/15769/download).
- **10A NCAC 27G .0209 for restrictive intervention and client rights:** Wrong. 27G .0209 covers medication requirements. Use 10A NCAC 27E .0107 for training in alternatives to restrictive interventions, and 27G .0202(g) for personnel training that includes client rights.[law.cornell.edu](https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/north-carolina/10A-N-C-Admin-Code-27G-0209?share_frome=aipt)
- **Main resource (oah.nc.gov) for training:** It is not a training resource. Use the DMH/DD/SUS approved-curricula list for de-escalation training.
- **30/60-day pass-through:** Obsolete. Both policies say Medicaid shall not require prior approval. The pass-through language remains only in CCP 8A revision history dated 01/01/2010 ([CCP 8A](https://medicaid.ncdhhs.gov/8a-enhanced-mental-health-and-substance-abuse-services/download?attachment)).
- **Service order signers:** The list is physician, physician assistant, nurse practitioner or licensed psychologist, valid twelve months. Timing before or on day one is correct. 8G has a different list and a nine-month term.
- **CCA within one year:** Not found in 8A-12, 8A-13 or 8G. The assessment must be done before service, and a substantially equivalent one may be reused.
- **Accreditation window months 9 to 12:** Under G.S. 122C-81(d) the on-site review must be completed by month 9. The CCPs set a one-year deadline from enrollment.
- **Accreditation deadline conflict:** The April 16, 2026 NC Medicaid bulletin indicates three years for SAIOP and SACOT, against one year in the CCPs. Both are reported above; the bulletin is the later document, but the CCP is the policy text.
- **CARF fee of $8,000 to $14,000:** NOT VERIFIED. CARF says the fee depends on surveyors, survey days and travel.
- **Accreditation applies to 8G too:** The app scopes Step 25 to 8A-12 and 8A-13, but CCP 8G also has accreditation language. Confirm 8G's requirement in the policy.
- **NCTracks alerts 70/50/20/5:** Consistent with the re-credentialing FAQs. An older NCTracks page mentions 45 days, which is superseded.
- **Monthly OIG/SAM screening:** Consistent with the Provider Administrative Participation Agreement. Add the NC Medicaid Excluded Providers list.
